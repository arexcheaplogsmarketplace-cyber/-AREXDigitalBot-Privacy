AREXDigitalBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

AREXDigitalBot ("the Bot") is operated by AREX Digital. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Bot.

1. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you use the Bot, we may receive basic Telegram information such as your Telegram user ID, username, first name, and messages or commands you send to the Bot.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide the Bot's features, process requests, provide customer support, prevent abuse, and improve the service.

3. Payments

If you purchase a product or service through the Bot, payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider. Payment providers may process payment information according to their own privacy policies. AREX Digital does not intentionally collect full card numbers through the Bot.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the Bot, process transactions, provide support, or comply with applicable law.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the Bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

The Bot may rely on Telegram and other third-party services. Their handling of your information is governed by their respective policies.

8. Your Choices

You may stop using the Bot at any time. If you have a privacy-related request, contact AREX Digital through the official support channel associated with the Bot.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions regarding AREXDigitalBot, please contact AREX Digital through the Bot's official support channel.
